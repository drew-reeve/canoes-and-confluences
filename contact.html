Common questions

How much does a presentation cost?

Fees depend on the length, format, and travel involved. Share a few details in the booking form and Laurie will reply with a quote.

What topics can you cover?

Any part of Pacific Northwest history. The programs listed are a starting point. Laurie is knowledgeable across the region's history, with a particular specialty in the age of exploration, and will shape a program to what you need.

How long are programs?

As long as you need. Anything from a focused 1 to 2 hour presentation on one subject to multi-day events with several presentations.

Who are programs for?

Everyone from 9 to 99. Laurie teaches K-12 classrooms, adult and community groups, cruise guests, and trip guests, and adapts each program to the people in front of her.

How far will Laurie travel?

Laurie is based in Lewiston, Idaho. She presents in person across the Pacific Northwest and will travel farther when travel costs are covered.

Do you offer online programs?

Sometimes. Get in touch with the details of your group and Laurie will let you know what's possible.

What do we need to provide?

Reasonable accommodations for the setting: a screen and projector where the space allows, or simply an open area for outdoor and field programs. Hosts also provide standard, reasonable compensation and, where needed, travel funding. Laurie brings her own hands-on materials, including the medicine chest.

How far ahead should we book?

As early as you can, especially for spring and summer dates. Shorter notice is sometimes possible, so it's always worth asking.

Can programs fit our curriculum?

Yes. School programs can be shaped around your grade level and your state's social studies standards. Mention what you're covering in the booking form.
